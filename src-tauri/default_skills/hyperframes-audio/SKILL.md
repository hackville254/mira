# HyperFrames Audio

Mixing for audio already placed in a HyperFrames composition.

## Scope

Use for: fade-in/fade-out, crossfade, track gain or volume, volume automation,
ducking, voiceover carve, track effects (EQ, compressor, limiter, gate,
saturation, delay, reverb, chorus, phaser, bitcrush), automation envelopes, and
`<hf-audio-group>` submix buses carrying a chain, a fader and an automation
clock for several tracks at once.

Do not use for sourcing or generating audio (BGM, SFX, voiceover), and not for
clip timing or track layout.
